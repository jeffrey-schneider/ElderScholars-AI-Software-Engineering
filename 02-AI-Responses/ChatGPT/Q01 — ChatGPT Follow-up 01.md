## One additional possibility: Obsidian Slides

Obsidian has a built-in Slides feature that can turn Markdown notes into presentations using slide separators.

For example:

```
# What Happens After the Vibe?

---

## AI Can Write the Code

But who tests it?

---

## The Engineering Lifecycle

- Requirements
- Design
- Implementation
- Testing
- Deployment
- Maintenance

---

## Can AI Perform Every Stage?
```

This gives you a useful middle ground. You can display prepared material as slides, then return to the underlying notes for exploration.

You could also use a Markdown presentation tool such as Marp later, without abandoning the repository.

## One concern: live demonstrations

Live AI demonstrations are compelling, but they are unpredictable.

An AI might take longer than expected, produce an incorrect answer, or become unavailable. NotebookLM might need time to process sources.

I would therefore prepare the first research experiment in advance and use live interaction to extend it.

That preserves spontaneity without making the lecture dependent on a successful real-time response.

## My recommendation

Use one GitHub repository, one Obsidian vault, and two presentation modes:

- Prepared mode: concise, readable slides explaining the concepts.
    
- Exploration mode: live navigation through Obsidian, AI tools, NotebookLM, GitHub, and generated software.
    

Keep the slides and the underlying research notes in the same repository. That way, the presentation itself becomes a version-controlled example of AI-assisted knowledge work.

There's also a teaching opportunity here that I particularly like: at the beginning, tell the audience that AI helped develop the presentation they're watching. At the end, show them the GitHub history and let them see exactly how it evolved.

One question that would help refine this recommendation: How long is your Elder Scholars presentation scheduled to run—50 minutes, 75 minutes, 90 minutes, or something else?

That will determine how much live exploration we can reasonably accommodate.
